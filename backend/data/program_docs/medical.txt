Eligibility: Medi-Cal provides free or low-cost health coverage to California residents
who meet income (and sometimes asset) requirements. Eligibility is primarily based on
Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) relative to the federal poverty level and household
size. Some categories (aged, blind, disabled) use non-MAGI rules including asset limits.

Application: Apply through Covered California or the county social services office.

Required documents: proof of income, proof of identity, proof of California residency.
