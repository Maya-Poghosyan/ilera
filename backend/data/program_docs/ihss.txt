Eligibility: IHSS helps eligible aged, blind, and disabled individuals who are unable to
remain safely in their own homes without assistance. To qualify, the recipient must:
- Be eligible for Medi-Cal.
- Reside in their own home (not a hospital, nursing facility, or board-and-care).
- Have a need for assistance with activities of daily living established by an in-home
  county social worker assessment.

How it works: Recipients are authorized a number of service hours per month. A caregiver
(who may be a family member) is paid by the program to provide the authorized services.

Application: Submit the SOC 295 application to the county IHSS/social services office. An
assessment visit is scheduled to determine authorized hours.

Required documents: SOC 295, Medi-Cal verification, proof of California residency.
