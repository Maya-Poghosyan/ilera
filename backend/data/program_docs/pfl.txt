Eligibility: PFL provides partial wage replacement to workers who take time off to care
for a seriously ill family member (or to bond with a new child). Requirements:
- Have paid into State Disability Insurance (SDI) through payroll deductions.
- Have a qualifying reason (caring for a seriously ill family member).
- Provide medical certification of the family member's condition.

Benefit: Up to 8 weeks of partial wage replacement within a 12-month period.

Application: File a PFL claim (DE 2501F) with the Employment Development Department (EDD).
