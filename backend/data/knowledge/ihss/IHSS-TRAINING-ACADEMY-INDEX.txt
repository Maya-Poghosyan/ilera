IHSS Training Academy
Welcome to the In-Home Supportive Services Training Academy (IHSSTA)! The IHSSTA is dedicated to instructing IHSS county staff through comprehensive training and resources. Our academy serves as a central hub for learning and development in the In-Home Supportive Services program, providing county staff with essential skills and knowledge to enhance the quality of care for our recipients. We offer specialized courses designed to improve IHSS county staff's abilities in conducting individual assessments and authorizing IHSS services.
