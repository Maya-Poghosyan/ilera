Find your Social Security benefit letter

A benefit verification letter can show your benefits, an application, or that you do not receive benefits.

  1. Open SSA’s benefit letter page.
  2. Follow SSA’s instructions to sign in and download your letter.
  3. For this intake, look for the benefit amount before deductions. Keep your Social Security number private.

If you need another way to get the letter, SSA’s page also explains how to request it by phone.

Prepared by Ilera from SSA guidance, checked October 14, 2026. Get your personal letter through SSA; this guide is not a benefit letter.
