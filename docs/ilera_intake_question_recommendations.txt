ILERA — INTAKE QUESTION ADDITIONS
California-first caregiver benefits navigation
Prepared October 12, 2026

PURPOSE
Keep the initial survey short. Add questions that identify benefits already received,
applications in progress, urgent needs, and barriers to next steps. Show conditional
questions only when relevant. Do not re-ask information already stored in CaseProfile.

PART 1 — 12 HIGH-PRIORITY QUESTIONS
(Use skip logic; these are not necessarily 12 additional screens.)

1. What kind of support would help you most right now?
   Type: Multi-select
   Answers: Getting paid as a caregiver; Help paying for care; In-home assistance;
   Medical coverage; Respite; Understanding existing benefits; Not sure.
   Purpose: Prioritize recommendations and actions, not exclude other programs.

2. Have you already applied for or received any benefits or care services for this person?
   Type: Single-select
   Answers: Yes; No; Not sure.
   Purpose: Distinguish new applicants from existing cases.

3. Which programs has the person receiving care already applied for or received?
   Type: Multi-select
   Answers: Medi-Cal; Medicare; IHSS; SSI; SSDI; Regional Center; VA benefits;
   Other (specify); Not sure.
   Trigger: Q2 = Yes or Not sure.
   Purpose: Track cross-program dependencies and avoid duplicate applications.

4. What is their current status with each selected program?
   Type: Per-program single-select
   Answers: Receiving benefits; Application submitted; Approved, waiting for services;
   Denied; Benefits stopped; Renewal due; Not sure.
   Trigger: One or more programs selected in Q3.
   Purpose: Choose start, follow-up, appeal, renewal, or verification actions.

5. Does the person receiving care currently have Medi-Cal coverage?
   Type: Single-select
   Answers: Yes; Applied, pending; No; Previously had it; Not sure.
   Trigger: Only if Q3/Q4 did not establish this.
   Purpose: Drive Medi-Cal/IHSS pathway.

6. Does the person receiving care currently have Medicare?
   Type: Single-select
   Answers: Yes, Original Medicare; Yes, Medicare Advantage; No; Not sure.
   Trigger: Only if not answered in existing intake/Q3.
   Purpose: Identify coverage and potential dual-eligibility context.

7. Where is the person receiving care currently living?
   Type: Single-select
   Answers: Own home; Family member's home; Assisted living; Nursing facility;
   Hospital/rehabilitation; Other (specify).
   Trigger: If not already collected.
   Purpose: Determine relevant home-care and transition pathways.

8. Is the person receiving care expected to move or leave a hospital or care facility soon?
   Type: Single-select
   Answers: Yes, within 7 days; Within 30 days; Later; No; Not sure.
   Trigger: Facility residence, hospitalization, or planned move.
   Purpose: Identify discharge/transition urgency.

9. What activities does the person receiving care need help with?
   Type: Multi-select
   Answers: Bathing; Dressing; Eating; Toileting; Walking/transfers; Medication
   management; Cooking; Cleaning; Shopping; Safety/supervision; Other.
   Trigger: If existing functional-needs fields are insufficient.
   Purpose: Guide care-needs and IHSS screening.

10. Does the person receiving care need supervision to avoid unsafe situations?
    Type: Single-select
    Answers: Yes, frequently; Sometimes; No; Not sure.
    Trigger: Cognitive issues or safety/supervision need.
    Purpose: Flag need for additional supervision assessment.

11. Have your caregiving responsibilities affected your employment or income?
    Type: Single-select (or multi-select if concurrent conditions matter)
    Answers: Stopped working; Reduced hours; Taking leave; Expect to take leave;
    No change; Not employed; Other.
    Purpose: Identify caregiver employment and wage-replacement pathways.

12. Is there anything time-sensitive about your situation?
    Type: Multi-select
    Answers: Care or housing at risk; Hospital discharge; Loss of income;
    Application deadline; Benefits ending; Immediate safety concern;
    Nothing urgent; Other.
    Purpose: Prioritize urgent action steps.

PART 2 — 15 CONDITIONAL FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS

EXISTING APPLICATIONS / BENEFITS
13. When did you submit your application or receive your most recent decision?
    Answers: Date; I don't remember.
    Trigger: Pending, denied, or recently changed status.

14. If benefits were denied, stopped, or reduced, did you receive a written notice
    explaining why?
    Answers: Yes; No; Not sure.
    Trigger: Denial, termination, or reduction.

15. Does the notice include a deadline to respond or appeal?
    Answers: Yes (collect deadline date); No; Not sure.
    Trigger: Written adverse decision.

16. Is there anything preventing you from moving forward with this application?
    Answers: Missing documents; Medical certification; Financial eligibility;
    Cannot reach agency; Waiting for appointment; Unclear instructions;
    Other; Nothing. Multi-select.
    Trigger: Existing application or application preparation.

MEDI-CAL / FINANCIAL PATHWAYS
17. Is the person receiving care married or in a registered domestic partnership?
    Answers: Yes; No; Widowed; Separated/divorced; Not sure.
    Trigger: If not already collected and financially relevant.

18. If married, does their spouse or partner live at home or in a care facility?
    Answers: At home; In a facility; Both in a facility; Other; Not sure.
    Trigger: Married and long-term-care pathway relevant.

19. Does the person receiving care own a home, additional property, or significant savings?
    Answers: Primary home; Other property; Savings/investments; None; Not sure.
    Multi-select.
    Trigger: Asset-tested Medi-Cal pathway; skip if already collected.

20. Has the person receiving care given away or transferred money or property
    within the last 30 months?
    Answers: Yes; No; Not sure; Prefer not to answer.
    Trigger: Nursing-facility long-term-care planning; not a universal gate.
    Purpose: Flag need for careful program-specific review, not disqualification.

IHSS / PROTECTIVE SUPERVISION
21. Does the person receiving care have difficulty recognizing dangerous situations
    or understanding how to stay safe?
    Answers: Yes; Sometimes; No; Not sure.
    Trigger: Safety/supervision concern.

22. Have they behaved in ways that could accidentally put them in danger?
    Answers: Wandering; Leaving appliances on; Unsafe use of objects;
    Other hazardous behavior; No; Not sure. Multi-select where applicable.
    Trigger: Cognitive or safety concerns.

23. Is there a doctor or licensed health professional who can assess and document
    their care needs?
    Answers: Yes; Need help finding one; No; Not sure.
    Trigger: IHSS application preparation.

CALIFORNIA PAID FAMILY LEAVE
24. When did you begin taking leave or losing wages because of caregiving?
    Answers: Date; Haven't started yet; Not sure.
    Trigger: Work interruption or leave expected.

25. Have you had California State Disability Insurance (CASDI/SDI) deductions
    from your paychecks during the last 18 months?
    Answers: Yes; No; Not sure.
    Trigger: Potential California PFL.

26. Can a medical professional certify that your family member has a serious
    health condition requiring care?
    Answers: Yes; Need to arrange this; No; Not sure.
    Trigger: Potential PFL care claim.

REGIONAL CENTER
27. Did the person's developmental condition or related difficulties begin
    before age 18?
    Answers: Yes; No; Not sure.
    Trigger: Developmental disability or childhood developmental concerns.

PART 3 — EXISTING FIELDS TO VERIFY BEFORE ADDING DUPLICATES
- Recipient date of birth / age.
- Recipient state, county, ZIP, and living arrangement.
- Caregiver relationship to recipient.
- Caregiver employment and relevant recent work history.
- Recipient income amounts, frequencies, and sources.
- Asset categories and approximate values.
- Health conditions AND actual daily functional limitations.
- Immigration/citizenship facts only where required for specific rules.
- Veteran/service member status.
- Existing care services.
- Whether the caregiver is authorized to assist with applications (collect
  documentation later when required).

PART 4 — IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITIES
P0: Add/review Q1–Q12, especially existing enrollment, program statuses,
    deadlines, living situation, care needs, and employment disruption.
P1: Add Q13–Q27 behind explicit conditional triggers.
P2: Collect detailed form-specific information through authenticated,
    action-scoped follow-up questionnaires.

DATA MODEL GUIDANCE
For each answer store: stable question ID, canonical CaseProfile field, value,
source (user-reported / document-extracted / agency-verified), last-confirmed
date, and optional supporting evidence.
Support "unknown" distinctly from "no"; never treat a skipped item as false.

Ask intake questions based on missing facts, not one questionnaire per program.
The action planner should distinguish:
- Eligibility-critical missing information
- Application-critical missing information
- Process-critical missing information

The eligibility preview should remain preliminary. Official agencies decide
eligibility and services; user-completed tasks do not automatically establish
that benefits were approved.
