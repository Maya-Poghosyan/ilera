## Roster attributes

`household.members` items contain `kind`, `relationship`, `age`, `lives_with_recipient`, `tax_link`, `employment_status`, `gross_monthly_income`, `income_sources`, `income_other`, and `income_varies`. No other household-member names or identifiers are required. Recipient appears once, spouse when married/partnered, and caregiver once (merged with spouse role when appropriate).

Derived `recipient.health_coverage`, `recipient.current_benefits`, `caregiver.has_income`, `recipient.has_income`, `recipient.household_size`, and `household.reported_income_monthly` are context values, not separately writable catalog questions. `tax.recipient_joint_return` is derived from `recipient.tax_filing_status`.
