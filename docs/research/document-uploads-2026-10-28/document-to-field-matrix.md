# Document-to-field matrix

Read with [the decision report](report.md). All rows are **optional lookup/extraction candidates**, not evidence the agency requires for screening. Field names refer to the inspected schema; `household.members[*].…` denotes roster attributes, not independent question IDs. “Component” means the document cannot complete the whole answer. Source quotations should be limited to the relevant lines, with page evidence. Recommendations about collection/freshness are Ilera design proposals, not agency retention or verification rules.

**Every row requires user confirmation before any answer is applied.** Confirm subject/role, applicable period and meaning even for directly printed facts. Accepting a document observation does not mean accepting a legal conclusion. The current schema has no general document evidence object; observations require a future separate contract.

Release tiers: **R1** first production scope after QA; **R1 conditional** only on relevant branch/unsure question; **R2** later after aggregation/reconciliation UX; **Exclude** from routine first-release intake, with manual lookup instead. All acquisition instructions assume the user already has lawful access; Ilera should not ask for account credentials.

## Collection exclusions, contradictions, freshness and required review

The exclusions below apply to extracted/stored model fields and to minimized submitted pages wherever feasible. Redacting later cannot undo initial disclosure. Keep name/role labels only as needed for explicit person matching; do not retain identifiers “just in case.” Mask barcodes and QR codes that encode identifiers, not just visible strings. User confirmation is **Yes for every row**, with the specific review listed here.

| ID | Sensitive material to exclude | Contradictions/staleness risk | User must confirm / minimum follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D01 | SSNs, EIN, control number, account/identity barcodes, unrelated deductions | W-2c supersedes a prior copy; duplicate employee copies; changed employer/plan/year | Person, correct year/correction, taxable versus excluded wages, current benefit offer/use; recipient allocation from other evidence. |
| D02 | All SSNs/ITINs, IP PINs, signatures, preparer credentials, direct-deposit routing/account, unrelated schedules | Draft/amended return; joint amounts; changed marital/claim plans; prior-year address | Filed year/status and purpose; same-year AGI versus planning estimate; current household/dependency plans independently. |
| D03 | Taxpayer/dependent/provider SSNs/ITINs/EINs, addresses not needed for screening, signatures | Multiple providers/recipients; amended return; cross-year payment; benefits excluded or carried | Which costs and benefits belong to this recipient/year, work purpose and actual provider relationship. |
| D04 | SSN, unrelated charitable/mortgage/tax details | Joint/family total, standard deduction instead, amended schedule | Recipient-only expense allocation, payer, reimbursements and whether reviewing filed deduction or planning. |
| D05 | EIN/SSN, bank details, unrelated clients/tenants/business addresses | Historical losses/current profits; cash/accrual timing; duplicate entities; multiple schedules | Time basis, owner share and current receipts/expenses; no forced negative-to-zero conversion. |

## Why exclusion is proportionate

Exact spouse asset IDs referenced as “spouse counterparts” above are listed here to avoid implying a generic prefix rewrite for every recipient field:

| --- | --- |
| `recipient.assets` | `recipient.spouse.assets` |
| `recipient.assets_savings_investments` | `recipient.spouse.assets_savings_investments` |
| `recipient.assets_cash` | `recipient.spouse.assets_cash` |
| `recipient.assets_other_property` | `recipient.spouse.assets_other_property` |
| `recipient.assets_additional_vehicles` | `recipient.spouse.assets_additional_vehicles` |
| `recipient.assets_retirement_accounts` | `recipient.spouse.assets_retirement_accounts` |
| `recipient.assets_life_insurance_cash_value` | `recipient.spouse.assets_life_insurance_cash_value` |

| `recipient.retirement_regular_payments` | `recipient.spouse.retirement_regular_payments` |
| `recipient.trust_annuity_details` | `recipient.spouse.trust_annuity_details` |

The separately gated VA forecast fields are `recipient.va_expected_annual_income`, `recipient.spouse.va_expected_annual_income`, and `recipient.va_unreimbursed_medical_expenses`. A bank statement cannot complete the Medi-Cal `recipient.recent_transfers`/`recipient.transfer_details` pair or the distinct VA `recipient.va_transfers_last_3_years`/`recipient.va_transfer_details` pair. The former is conditioned on nursing-home residence or contemplated nursing-home care; the latter is inside the VA pension pathway. Neither has a generic spouse-prefix copy.

An SSN, Medicare number, bank account number or immigration document number is not an intake question in the inspected catalog. Collecting one to extract a date/amount adds exposure without resolving the screening criterion. SOC 426 is a provider enrollment workflow with its own identity documentation, rather than a reason to collect the same packet at preliminary screening. [CDSS SOC 426, instructions and enrollment certification](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/cdssweb/entres/forms/english/soc426.pdf). Employment I-9 verification likewise has specific acceptable-document/employee-choice rules; Ilera intake should not masquerade as that process. [USCIS I-9 instructions](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/forms/i-9instr.pdf).

Use short self-report first for current citizenship/category, work authorization, household relationship/residence, support share, goals, expected leave and care needs. Existing documents can help an unsure person look up facts, without becoming mandatory uploads. Some agency applications legitimately require identifiers or certifications downstream; this recommendation concerns completing Ilera's production **screening intake**.
