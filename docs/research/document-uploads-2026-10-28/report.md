# Document assistance for Ilera intake

Research date: October 28, 2026. Decision proposal, not an implementation or an agency eligibility determination.

**Recommendation:** offer optional, targeted document assistance backed by one shared case document collection. First support recent pay statements, current benefit letters, selected coverage evidence, and a small tax-review bundle. Keep the three existing title cards, add contextual entry points in Coverage, and reuse an uploaded document across sections only after confirming person, period, and purpose. Do not make immigration papers, bank statements, full returns, or medical records routine intake requirements.

The main value is helping caregivers find unfamiliar numbers and dates. Documents do not replace their description of care, household relationships, employment changes, future plans, or expenses paid for a particular recipient. Prior-year returns are useful for reviewing that return, but often poor evidence for current benefits screening or planning another year.

## What the current application actually asks

Inspected the working checkout, including its uncommitted intake changes. The source catalog is `backend/app/intake/schema.py`, not a generic benefits questionnaire. It has **145 top-level question records**: About You 11; recipient identity 15; Care Needs 10; Work and Caregiving 21; Household and Assets 34; Tax Information 18; Coverage and Benefits 36. Conditional visibility means no caregiver sees all 145. `household.members` is one question containing ten attributes per member, so the catalog count understates its effort.

The frontend gets the backend schema. `frontend/src/lib/intake-flow.ts` filters visible questions before grouping pages and adds seven section introductions; `intake-schema.ts` computes ages, shared address, filing relationships and coverage aliases. `question-field.tsx` uses the selected tax year in residence guidance. `page.tsx` provides browser drafts, missing-answer review and section document/location guides. The three proposed tax choices—**Upload documents / Enter manually / Skip tax savings for now**—and document uploads are not implemented. No tax entry-choice field ID exists today; this report does not invent one as an existing field.

## Program and tax findings that change the decision

**IHSS and Medi-Cal.** A current county notice can reduce confusion about enrollment or an application decision. SOC 873 is required before IHSS services authorization; it is not a universal prerequisite to answer a screening questionnaire. County needs assessment determines authorized services. Avoid asking a new applicant to create a certification just to finish intake. [CDSS IHSS](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/in-home-supportive-services), [SOC 873, pp. 1–2](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/cdssweb/entres/forms/english/soc873.pdf).

DHCS currently describes asset testing on specified pathways from January 2026, with $130,000 for one person through June 2027 and a different schedule from July 2027. Household residents do not necessarily equal the eligibility unit. Retirement funds receiving regular payments and transfers have special treatment; its current FAQ excludes pre-January-2026 transfers from the described long-term-care look-back. This makes dated ownership/payment records potentially useful **after** identifying a relevant pathway; it does not justify bank statements from everyone. Do not reuse an earlier audit's asset threshold or transfer cutoff without rechecking it. [DHCS asset FAQ, “What is an asset limit?” and transfer sections](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/medi-cal/help/asset-limit-frequently-asked-questions/).

**Paid leave versus job protection.** EDD specifies wage loss, working/looking for work at leave onset, and at least $300 in relevant base-period earnings subject to SDI; citizenship/immigration does not affect PFL eligibility. A CASDI line is useful evidence of a deduction in that pay period. It does not establish every PFL requirement or sufficient wages in the correct quarters. [EDD eligibility](https://edd.ca.gov/en/disability/Am_I_Eligible_for_PFL_Benefits). EDD's computation notice estimates benefits; it is not proof payments were received. Use actual benefit payment history for prior usage. [EDD review documents](https://edd.ca.gov/pflreviewdocuments).

For a concrete timing example, EDD's standard base period for a claim beginning November 2, 2026 is July 1, 2025–June 30, 2026. A current October 2026 paystub would miss that period. One clearly matched historical stub with at least $300 in explicitly SDI-covered wages could support the threshold Yes; a No generally needs sufficiently complete records, and weekly benefit estimates need quarterly totals. EDD lists special-base-period circumstances too. [EDD base-period guidance](https://edd.ca.gov/en/disability/Calculating_PFL_Benefit_Payment_Amounts/). The actual DE 2501F instructions distinguish the claimant's statement from the care recipient's statement and clinician certification, and direct voluntary-plan claimants to their employer. Those are application tasks, not documents everyone must upload to finish screening. [DE 2501F, instruction pp. 1–2](https://edd.ca.gov/siteassets/files/pdf_pub_ctr/de2501f.pdf).

WH-381 communicates FMLA eligibility/rights and WH-382 designation; neither is a payroll award. CFRA has its own rules, including a five-employee employer threshold, rather than FMLA's worksite test. The current field `caregiver.employer_size_75_miles` cannot fully resolve statewide employer size for CFRA. Documents cannot cure an absent structured question. Federal employee rules also need their own pathway. [DOL forms](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fmla/forms), [CRD employment](https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/Employment/), [CRD leave resources](https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/family-medical-pregnancy-leave/).

**Medicare and Social Security.** A Medicare card expressly shows A/B parts and start dates; its number is unnecessary for this intake. Confirm coverage is still active. [Medicare card guidance](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/using-medicare/your-medicare-card). A current SSA verification letter can describe receipt, pending application, or nonreceipt; classify the actual wording. A historic award's disability entitlement date must not be confused with application date, first bank deposit, or disability onset. [SSA benefit letter](https://www.ssa.gov/manage-benefits/get-benefit-letter). Under-65 Medicare pathways differ for SSDI, ALS and ESRD, so diagnosis or one benefit amount is not sufficient to infer a coverage start date. [Medicare other paths](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/other-paths).

**VA.** Prefer a short benefit/service summary or current award to a complete disability claim file. A rating supports the rating bucket but does not prove PCAFC eligibility: VA also lists health enrollment, discharge, personal-care duration and caregiver conditions. [VA PCAFC](https://www.va.gov/family-and-caregiver-benefits/health-and-disability/comprehensive-assistance-for-family-caregivers/). Pension is a separate service/income/net-worth pathway, and spouse resources matter. Do not turn a compensation award into pension receipt or the veteran's benefit into caregiver pay. [VA pension eligibility](https://www.va.gov/pension/eligibility/). The intake asks forward-looking 12-month VA income and expenses; historical statements are components for a user-approved forecast, not direct answers.

**W-2 Box 10.** It reports dependent-care assistance, potentially including employee salary reduction, employer contributions and in-kind care. It can include elected amounts under grace-period reporting and forfeitures. It does not identify the care recipient, qualified expenses, actual reimbursement for their care, or current plan availability. Blank/zero does not mean no offer. Never copy it directly into `tax.care_reimbursements` or choose “Yes, and I use it” for the current employer without confirmation. [W-2 instructions, Box 10](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw2w3).

Year-specific caution: 2026 Pub. 15-B gives a $7,500 dependent-care exclusion ($3,750 married separately). The fetched 2026 W-2 instructions still refer to a $5,000 exclusion in the Box 10 passage; 2025 Form 2441 instructions concern 2025. Record this source discrepancy, use year-specific controlling guidance before calculations, and never encode a threshold from a form label or a prior-year corpus passage. Extraction needs the **reported amount**, not a legal conclusion about exclusion. [2026 Pub. 15-B, dependent-care assistance](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15b), [2025 Form 2441 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i2441).

**Tax documents are strongest for same-year review.** A complete filed 1040 supports reported filing status and AGI; 2025 has AGI on line 11a and its continuation on 11b. A caregiver's joint AGI is not the recipient's separate gross income. Dependency listed on a prior return does not prove support, residence or claim plans for another year. [2025 Form 1040, pp. 1–2](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f1040--2025.pdf), [IRS dependency tests](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p501).

Form 2441 identifies reported care expenses/benefits but can cover multiple people and providers; reconcile recipient-specific allocations. The dependent-care credit has work, qualifying-person and residence requirements beyond ordinary dependency. [2025 Form 2441, Parts I–III](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f2441--2025.pdf), [IRS Pub. 503](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p503). Schedule A medical totals can cover the taxpayer, spouse and dependents collectively. They cannot be copied into `tax.medical_expense_amount`, which asks about the recipient. Itemizing, qualifying unreimbursed payments and the 7.5% AGI floor affect the deduction; a receipt total does not prove savings. [Schedule A, lines 1–4](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f1040sa--2025.pdf), [IRS Pub. 502](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p502).

California's FTB 3506 has separate requirements, including California care and federal AGI limits. The current schema does not establish where every expense was incurred; CA residence alone cannot establish this. Do not promise a state credit from federal Form 2441. [2025 FTB 3506 instructions](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-3506-instructions.html). For public caregiving payments, Notice 2014-7 requires program/home facts; a W-2 alone does not establish exclusion. [IRS waiver-payment Q&A](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/certain-medicaid-waiver-payments-may-be-excludable-from-income).

## Recommended upload grouping and tax entry

Keep **Work and Caregiving → Household and Assets → Tax Information** with their title cards. Offer “Use a document to help answer” at a relevant question/card and open the **same shared document collection**. Coverage needs an entry point too. A forced shared upload step at the start would ask for documents before Ilera knows the person, relevant branch or benefit, increasing collection. Three isolated upload buckets would cause repeated paystub/W-2 uploads and inconsistent extractions. The recommended hybrid is one underlying collection with contextual entry points and explicit cross-section review.

1. Establish person, current household and intent through brief self-report. A caregiver may upload a recipient document only with appropriate authority; no authorization can be inferred from having a file.
2. At a useful branch, name the optional document and the exact facts it can help with. Keep manual entry and unsure available.
3. Reuse a document observation with its owner/date attached. Example: current paystub suggests one roster income component and CASDI; W-2 contributes historical tax facts. Do not silently project either across time periods.
4. At Tax Information, use the proposed three choices. First distinguish **review a filed year** from **estimate/plan an unfiled year**. Upload is optional in both. The selected year is the caregiver's decision, not whichever year the parser finds.
5. “Skip tax savings for now” should mark the tax-savings workflow deferred, preserving household facts and document evidence useful to other programs. Retain `tax.dependent_care_fsa_available` in Work; skipping should not erase an employer benefit answer. Public-payment/program/residence observations can remain available to benefits screening even if the tax analysis is deferred. No tax computation should run just because such evidence exists.

Explicitly retain even on a tax skip: recipient marital status; spouse living arrangement; recipient federal filing/dependency plans; caregiver claim plans; other claimant relationship; recipient dependent-claim plans; household members, relationships, ages, residence/tax links, current employment, income sources/amounts/variation; assets and conditional spouse/transfer details. These inform program-specific units and financial pathways. Do not treat “skip tax savings” as no tax household, no dependents, zero income, or a refusal to answer benefits questions.

The schema's household filing questions lack their own explicit reference period although tax questions use `tax.year`; future UX needs a clear current/coverage-year household basis and separate historical tax facts. Similarly, tax support/residence and inability-to-care tests may need richer follow-up. Adding uploads should not claim these semantic gaps are solved.

## Extraction and review experience

The uploader should ask who the document is about and its period, preview selected pages, allow masking/cropping, and show the intended questions. Do not use SSNs, bank account numbers or immigration numbers for matching. If masking is promised before processing, it must occur before server/model transmission; a display overlay or post-model cleanup is not redaction.

Review suggestions beside the source page/crop. Each must state “document says,” owner, date/year, period/unit, and whether it is direct text, a calculation, or a question to confirm. Example: “CASDI $X withheld on pay date Y—have your paychecks had SDI deductions?” versus “PFL eligible.” Provide Accept / Edit / Not this person / Not current / Unsure. Batch acceptance may be offered for clear low-risk values, with amount/owner/period always visible. User confirmation is required for every applied intake answer; confidence alone never waives it.

For missing/cropped values, return null with a reason. Distinguish absent field, unreadable, redacted, out of date and genuinely zero. A blank Box 10 is not zero offer; one missing 1099 is not no other income. Explain the smallest missing fact, allow manual entry, and never block all intake because a file is unreadable.

For contradictions, retain both observations and the user's answer. Compare only like person/period/measure; a paystub gross and bank net deposit are not a conflict. A historical award and current termination notice may describe a timeline. Newer upload time is not newer effective evidence. Ask a targeted question, record which observation was accepted, and leave the affected criterion unresolved where necessary. Never overwrite an externally resolved fact through the intake mapper. After confirmed parent edits, run existing visibility/cleanup and show changed branches; keep original observations outside answers so hidden descendants do not become stale facts later.

Freshness is a proposed product rule, not an agency standard: paystubs from the latest ordinary pay cycle (prefer enough cycles to represent variability); current-year SSA/pension award plus latest change letter; latest effective coverage notice; current employer plan year and the individual's election period; current VA summary and later decisions. Reconfirm “still true today” for ongoing facts. Historical tax evidence must match `tax.year`; filing date, upload date, service date and payment date remain separate. Prior PFL usage needs the complete relevant rolling 12 months; forecasts require explicit user approval of expected changes. Do not apply an arbitrary 30/90-day expiration to a Medicare start date or completed historical return.

Unsupported inferences explicitly prohibited:

- Document possession → identity verified, consent, legal authority, or current residence.
- W-2/paystub → citizenship, work authorization, tax residency, all income, current job, work state from employer address, or gross household total.
- Box 10 blank → benefit not offered; positive → current use, this recipient's expenses, actual reimbursements, or tax eligibility.
- Guide → enrolled; enrollment → reimbursements paid; annual election → claims reimbursed.
- Current pay/annual W-2 → PFL base-period wages, rolling hours, leave wage loss, overlapping payment schedule, or employer size.
- EDD computation → paid benefits; WH-382 → PFL award; short benefit letter → complete prior 12-month usage.
- Coverage card/1095 → active coverage now; VA rating → health enrollment, pension, PCAFC approval or six-month care need.
- Return dependent → current claim plan, support share, half-year residence or all residents; 1040 joint AGI → recipient gross income; SS benefits total → tax-test gross income.
- Schedule A total → caregiver's recipient-specific medical amount; invoice/EOB → paid/unreimbursed/qualified expense; receipt → work purpose/provider relationship.
- 1099 gross receipts → net self-employment income; 1099-R annual distribution → monthly pension or remaining retirement assets.
- Bank balance/deposit/withdrawal → countable assets, gross income, ownership share, gift, prohibited transfer or complete financial picture.
- Immigration card expiration/category → current work permission, continuous residence or tax residence without relevant exceptions/history.

## Architecture comparison and evidence boundary

No resources were provisioned or application code changed. Production document processing should use the **dedicated Azure OpenAI resource** requested by the user, separate from eligibility research. Do not inherit historical email-design text proposing reuse of a general account. Keep private user documents out of the public policy corpus, eligibility retrieval index, reference guides and application logs. Specialists should receive reviewed facts plus evidence references, not arbitrary raw personal documents.

| Approach | Good use | Practical limits / recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local deterministic PDF/form/text parsing | Known digital W-2/1040 layouts, labeled fields, repeatable year-specific normalization | Lowest data exposure for extracted text; scanned PDFs need OCR. Validate hidden text against visible page, form edition, duplicate copies and amount geometry. Local parsing does not guarantee correct meaning. Start here for recognizable digital forms. |
| OCR / Azure Document Intelligence | Scanned stubs/notices; text, tables, page coordinates; available prebuilt tax/paystub models | Extra processing boundary and OCR errors; prebuilt support varies by subtype/version. Use layout evidence and validators, not document-type confidence as legal proof. [Microsoft model overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/document-intelligence/model-overview?view=doc-intel-4.0.0). |
| Direct PDF/image model input | Visual layouts that plain text loses; exception review and varied notices | Larger page exposure and token use; page-citation output can be fabricated. OpenAI documents text plus page-image handling; Azure documents PDF input for Responses, but exact deployed model/region/API support must be verified independently. [OpenAI file inputs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/file-inputs), [Azure Responses PDF input](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/how-to/responses). |
| Structured model extraction over selected text/crops | Normalize candidate observations into a constrained schema and identify ambiguities | Schema shape is not factual accuracy. Require nulls, evidence validation, deterministic calculations and user review. Do not ask the model to decide eligibility or infer missing facts. [Microsoft extraction example](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/developer/ai/how-to/extract-entities-using-structured-outputs). |

Recommended pipeline: case-authorized upload → file validation/quarantine and page selection → deterministic parse, OCR only where needed → minimal relevant text/crops to dedicated document model if needed → allowlisted observations with source spans → deterministic unit/date/enum checks → user review → answer/fact projection with provenance. No browsing, tool execution or arbitrary instructions from uploaded text. Reject documents' embedded instructions as data. Require idempotency, safe retry, case isolation and deletion across originals, derived text/images, OCR results, model state and app backups according to the adopted retention policy.

Vendor statements need configuration checks. Microsoft says Azure-hosted model inputs/outputs are not available to OpenAI and are not used to train foundation models without permission. It separately describes stateful storage, deployment-geography differences and potential abuse-review handling. Those are **not** a promise that every request is unretained or never seen by a reviewer; a dedicated resource alone supplies no such guarantee. Confirm the document resource's actual deployment type, monitoring approval, API state settings and contracts before publishing privacy language. [Microsoft model data handling](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/openai/data-privacy).

Document Intelligence currently documents 24-hour input/result retention after analysis and an earlier Delete Analyze Result operation. That does not delete Ilera's blob, logs, queues or other model files. Implement and verify those lifecycles separately. [Document Intelligence data handling](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/document-intelligence/data-privacy-security). Suggested MVP policy: avoid persistent model files/vector stores where unnecessary; delete rejected uploads promptly and raw originals after review under a clearly disclosed, approved retention period. No specific retention duration or regulatory compliance is claimed as already implemented.

The illustrative JSON contract separates observations from answer candidates. Required concepts: case/document/page identity; document class/edition/issuer; subject role and explicit user match; statement/tax/effective/service/payment dates; source quote and page region/span; raw and normalized values with units/gross-vs-net; direct versus derived status and calculation inputs; missing/conflict reasons; extraction version; review and evidence provenance. No extracted secret identifiers. Page evidence must be checked against the actual rendered/text source; hallucinated citations invalidate that candidate. Profile evidence should continue to point to a retained audit record if the raw file is deleted, with disclosure that the original is no longer available.

No new answers/evidence were produced. Existing October 12 documents contain useful leads but include unverified dates, overcollection and citation issues already noted by their audit. They were not used to establish current policy. Repository network notes describe private database access requirements; no network/public-access change was made.
